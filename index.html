// About Me

A Mind That Never Stops Questioning

Driven by curiosity and fuelled by the
desire to understand the world.

I'm Hamza — a blogger, thinker, and technology learner who believes every idea deserves a platform. My digital journey started with a single question: how does this all work?

From the cosmos to code, from philosophy to practical web development, I explore the spectrum of human knowledge. This website is my living archive — a record of where I've been, what I've thought, and where I'm headed.

I believe in self-growth, the power of consistent learning, and the idea that creativity is the highest form of intelligence. Every blog post, every project, every experiment is a step forward.

// Journey

The Story So Far

Every great journey begins with a single spark of curiosity.

🌱
Early Years
Childhood Curiosity

Born with an insatiable need to understand the "why" behind everything. Books, stars, and nature were my first teachers. The universe felt like a puzzle built just for me.

📚
School Years
The Academic Awakening

School gave me structure, but my real education happened outside the textbooks. Science fairs, writing competitions, and late-night reading sessions shaped a curious intellect.

🌐
Discovery Era
First Internet Experience

The internet opened a universe within a universe. Suddenly every question had a potential answer. I spent hours exploring science forums, philosophy debates, and technology blogs.

💻
Tech Era
Learning Web Development

HTML, CSS, JavaScript — the languages of the web became my new vocabulary. I discovered that coding is not just logic; it's creative expression in its purest digital form.

✍️
Creative Era
Blogging Begins

Words became my weapon of choice. I started writing about science, technology, human psychology, and my own philosophy. Each post became a conversation with the world.

🚀
Now & Beyond
Building the Future

The mission is clear: build, create, inspire. I'm working on larger digital projects, expanding my blogging reach, and exploring the intersection of AI, philosophy, and human potential.
